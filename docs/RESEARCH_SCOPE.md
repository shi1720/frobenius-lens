# Research scope and claim ledger

## Main result

For scalar, one-dimensional, translation-invariant linear rules over a **prime field**, with radius bounded by r, exact **full-ring** snapshots at times p^a from known seeds have a complete rule-ambiguity certificate. For experiment e=(n,t,s), let q=(x^n−1)/gcd(x^n−1,s). Divide each irreducible multiplicity in q by t and round upward to form Q_e. Let L be the lcm of all Q_e. A normalized coefficient difference h is invisible exactly when L divides h. Nullity is max(0,2r+1−deg L).

This follows from established quotient-ring, Frobenius, and ideal arguments. The manuscript gives a complete proof and identifies these antecedents. We do not claim invention of polynomial CA representations, repeated-root algebra, Krylov dependence, or modular Gaussian elimination.

## What the experiments establish

- Rank agreement on 26,180 exhaustive small design configurations.
- Rank agreement on 600 mixed suites.
- Exactly predicted fibers in 435 complete rule enumerations.
- A resolved radius-four example in which the original suite has fiber size 128 and a nine-cell repair has size one.
- Separate symbolic, direct-simulation, and binary-browser paths.

These are conclusive within the specified finite classes. They do not measure language-model failure rates, naturally occurring software bugs, human usability, or market adoption.

## What is outside the guarantee

Arbitrary source code, nonlinearity, unknown seeds, stochastic updates, noisy or partial observations, unknown origin/reflection, matrix-valued rules, multidimensional lattices, and times not p^a. The API rejects unsupported time parameters instead of silently applying the theorem.

## Novelty assessment

The proposed contribution is the complete seed-aware **joint design audit plus explicit repair workflow**, packaged as an independently replayable mathematical exploration tool. A bounded literature search found related classical algebra, CA identification, repeated-root coding theory, and evaluator design, but did not establish an identical audit-and-repair artifact. Absence from search results is not a proof of priority.

The work is well aligned with an application/showcase workshop on AI-assisted mathematical exploration. It is not presented as a breakthrough in general LLM architecture or as an accepted archival conference paper. A more ambitious archival submission would benefit from independent specialist assessment and real simulation-revision case studies.
